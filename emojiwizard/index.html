Emoji Wizard

A Spellcasting Puzzle Quest

Cast emoji spells to solve enchanted puzzles in this magical adventure. Journey through four acts of increasingly challenging levels as you master new spells and unravel the mystery of the Emoji Realm.

Coming soon to the App Store and Google Play

Features

  • 4 Acts — Journey through distinct magical realms with unique themes
  • Story-Driven — Fully voiced narrative with memorable characters
  • Unique Spells — Learn and combine emoji spells to clear enchanted tiles
  • Immersive Audio — Sound design brings the world to life (headphones recommended)

How to Play

  • Select spells from your spell panel and add them to your queue
  • Cast your spell queue to transform tiles on the grid
  • Match and clear all target tiles to complete each level
  • Progress through the story and unlock new acts
